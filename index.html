01 / ABOUT

Localization and mapping in complex environments.

My doctoral research is in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing at the College of Surveying and Geo-Informatics, Tongji University. I completed my Ph.D. defense on August 15, 2026.

My doctoral supervisor at Tongji University is Prof. Hangbin Wu. During my visiting doctoral research at the University of Twente’s Faculty ITC, I was supervised by Dr. Ville V. Lehtola (Assistant Professor) and Prof. George Vosselman.

My research focuses on robust multi-sensor perception, localization, mapping, and map updating for autonomous driving and mobile robots in complex environments. My interests include LiDAR–inertial state estimation, dynamic-scene mapping, degeneracy-aware localization, multi-session map fusion, and point cloud change detection.
