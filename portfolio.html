01 / CALIBRATION

Online LiDAR–IMU calibration

Challenge

Limited sensor excitation makes extrinsic parameters difficult to estimate reliably.

Approach

Nonuniform motion modeling and excitation analysis support staged estimation of rotation, translation, and temporal offset.

Sensor fusion · Observability · State estimation

Related publication: On-the-Fly Extrinsic Calibration of LiDAR–IMU

IEEE TIM · 2025

02 / DYNAMIC SCENES

Clean maps from moving platforms

Challenge

Moving objects contaminate static maps and can interfere with odometry.

Approach

A two-pass pipeline couples moving-object removal with LiDAR–inertial state estimation to reconstruct cleaner, more consistent urban maps.

LiDAR–inertial odometry · Moving-object removal · Point clouds

Related publication: Clean and Consistent MLS Point Clouds from the Start

ISPRS JPRS · 2026

03 / ROBUST LOCALIZATION

Localization in tunnels and corridors

Challenge

Repetitive geometry leaves some motion directions weakly constrained.

Approach

Geometric and intensity residuals with adaptive weighting strengthen localization in an iterative error-state Kalman filter.

IESKF · Geometry · Intensity features

Related publication: LiDAR–Inertial Mapping in Degenerate Scenes

Chinese Journal of Lasers · 2026

04 / LONG-TERM MAPPING

Multi-session map fusion

Challenge

Repeated mapping introduces partial overlap, accumulated drift, and temporal scene changes.

Approach

Inter-session alignment, fusion, and point cloud change detection support the analysis of crowdsourced mobile mapping data.

Map alignment · Map fusion · Change detection
