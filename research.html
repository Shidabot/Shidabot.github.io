01

Online LiDAR–IMU calibration under weak excitation

Nonuniform motion modeling, excitation analysis, and staged estimation of LiDAR–IMU rotation, translation, and temporal offset.

02

Dynamic-scene LiDAR–inertial mapping

A two-pass LiDAR–inertial odometry pipeline couples moving-object removal with state estimation to reconstruct cleaner, more consistent static urban maps.

03

Robust localization in geometrically degenerate scenes

Geometric and intensity residuals with adaptive weighting in an iterative error-state Kalman filter strengthen constraints in weakly observed directions in corridors, tunnels, and repetitive structures.

04

Multi-session map fusion and point cloud change detection

Inter-session alignment, map fusion, and change detection for crowdsourced mobile mapping data with partial overlap, odometry drift, and temporal scene changes.
